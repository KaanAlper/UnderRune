Development previews

Clips from the local development build. PLAY currently includes Jevil, opening Lancer, standalone Clover and both K.Round encounters, plus Jevil attack practice. Other encounters shown here are previews.

Choose your signal. Prepare your party.

The theater

Red curtains and warm lights for UNDERTALE; blue curtains and green lights for DELTARUNE. Pick a remote and watch its theme fill the stage. DELTARUNE opens the device menu. UNDERTALE is coming soon.

Your party, your loadout

Choose a character, equip a weapon and two armor pieces, and pack a 12-slot bag. Search equipment and compare stats. Your selections stay saved on your device.

Fight, act, survive

Jevil, Lancer, Clover and K.Round open with your party and a command menu. Time your attacks, cast spells, use packed items, and defend through enemy turns. Jevil also has 16 separate practice patterns. Visuals, sounds and special equipment effects are still being compared with the game.

Pick up the controller

Press PLAY above to enter the theater. Select DELTARUNE’s remote, connect through the TV, and open Chapter 1. Browse boss cards with left/right or swipe, then choose Prepare party. Jevil, opening Lancer, standalone Clover and both K.Round fights are ready to play. Equip your party, pack items, then press START BATTLE. Select PRACTICE separately to try one Jevil attack.

Use arrow keys or WASD to select commands and move. Z or Enter confirms; X goes back or slows movement during attacks. P pauses; R restarts the battle. Touch controls are available on phones. Other encounters have equipment previews while their browser battles are being built.
